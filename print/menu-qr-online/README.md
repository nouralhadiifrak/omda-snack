# Online menu QR sticker

`menu-qr-online-print.pdf` is the print file: 100 × 100 mm sticker plus 3 mm bleed (page 106 × 106 mm).
`menu-qr-online-300dpi.png` is the same at 300 dpi.

The QR opens `https://al-omda.ma/menu`, a small page (`menu/index.html`) that forwards to the menu on
the website, with photos and WhatsApp ordering. To send the stickers somewhere else later, change that
page; the printed stickers stay valid. Error correction is H, so the logo in the middle doesn't stop it scanning.
